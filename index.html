Tuan Nguyen 🇻🇳

Contact Me

I am currently a fourth-year Ph.D. student (2023–present) in a joint Ph.D. program between VinUniversity and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC). I work at the Security and Artificial Intelligence Lab (SAIL) and the VinUni-Illinois Smart Health Center (VISHC), co-advised by Prof. Kok-Seng Wong and Prof. Khoa D. Doan.

I am also pursuing a Master's degree in Computer Science at UIUC, starting Fall 2025, under the supervision of Prof. Fan Lai, with a research focus on Efficient Machine Learning, especially in Multi-Agent Systems, Large Language Models (LLMs), and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

My broader research interests include:

  • Trustworthy Machine Learning
  • Efficient and Scalable Machine Learning Systems

I have developed a research papers website where I curate and share a list of research papers, code, and resources related to these areas.

Previously, I earned my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the VNU University of Engineering and Technology.

If you're interested in my research, feel free to reach out via email for communication or collaboration.

Current Research

RAG Compression & Efficient LLM Systems

I am exploring data-mining-driven retrieval-augmented generation compression: mining historical query-document-model interactions into reusable, document-keyed evidence views that preserve the standard RAG interface while reducing online latency, KV-cache pressure, and token cost.
